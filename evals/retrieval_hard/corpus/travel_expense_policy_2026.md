# Corporate Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy (Active 2026)

> **STATUS: CURRENT ACTIVE POLICY - EFFECTIVE JANUARY 2026**
> 
> *Notice: This document establishes the mandatory corporate travel expense guidelines under policy code `EXP-CURR-2026`. This policy supersedes all prior travel guidelines including the archived 2023 policy.*

---

## 1. Scope and Core Guidelines

All full-time and contract personnel traveling on behalf of ORBIT must adhere to standard fiscal limits established by finance. Travel requests must be submitted through the modern booking platform `OrbitVoyage` using client account routing code `VOY-440`. Any booking made outside `VOY-440` requires manual routing via finance code `FIN-APP-02`.

Employees should exercise prudent judgment when incurring travel expenses. Business travel must be pre-authorized by an immediate team lead at least 14 calendar days prior to departure.

---

## 2. Daily Per Diem Allowances

Under policy `EXP-CURR-2026`, daily meal per diems are strictly fixed at $85.00 per day for domestic travel. International per diems vary by country tier. Alcohol and tobacco purchases are strictly non-reimbursable.

| Category | Daily Maximum Allowance | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Breakfast | $20.00 | Only if hotel does not provide complimentary breakfast |
| Lunch | $25.00 | Midday meal during business hours |
| Dinner | $40.00 | Evening meal while on travel status |
| **Total Daily Per Diem** | **$85.00** | Maximum combined daily limit |

---

## 3. Lodging and Ground Transportation

Hotel accommodations must be selected from standard tier corporate preferred hotels:

- **Tier 1 Major Metro Lodging Cap**: Maximum allowable rate is $280.00 per night excluding taxes (applies to New York, San Francisco, London, and Tokyo).
- **Standard Region Lodging Cap**: Maximum allowable rate is $195.00 per night excluding taxes.
- **Mileage Reimbursement**: Personal vehicle mileage reimbursement rate is $0.67 per mile.
- **Ground Transit**: Standard taxi fares, airport shuttle buses, and rideshare rides are reimbursable between transit hubs and hotel.

---

## 4. Documentation and Approval Thresholds

Employees must submit expense claims via the modern ERP portal `OrbitFinance` within 30 days of trip conclusion:

1. **Receipt Requirement Threshold**: Itemized receipts are required for any individual expense exceeding $25.00.
2. **Manager Approval Limit**: Department manager approval is required for any expense report exceeding $500.00.
3. **Cash Advances**: Advances are capped at $500.00 per trip upon electronic request via corporate debit card reload.
