# Employee Benefits, Paid Leave, and Wellness Policy

> **HUMAN RESOURCES GUIDELINE: HR-BEN-404**
> 
> *Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Eligibility: All Regular Full-Time Employees*

---

## 1. Annual Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leave Allotments

ORBIT believes in fostering sustainable work practices and healthy work-life integration. Regular full-time staff accrue paid time off dynamically across the corporate calendar year:

| Leave Category | Annual Entitlement | Accrual Frequency | Carryover Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paid Time Off (PTO) | 25 business days | 2.08 days per month | Maximum 5 days carryover into following year (must use by March 31) |
| Paid Sick Leave | 10 business days | Frontloaded Jan 1 | No annual carryover; resets each year |
| Parental Leave | 16 weeks fully paid | Per qualifying event | Must be taken within 12 months of birth or adoption |
| Bereavement Leave | 5 business days | Per qualifying event | Immediate family compassionate leave |

Employees must log planned time off in the HR portal `OrbitPeople` at least two weeks in advance of vacations exceeding three consecutive days.

---

## 2. Wellness Stipend and Physical Wellbeing

To encourage holistic health and preventive care, ORBIT provides each eligible employee an annual wellness reimbursement stipend totaling $1,200.00 per calendar year:

1. **Quarterly Claim Schedule**: The annual wellness allotment is divided into four quarterly claims of up to $300.00 each. Unclaimed quarterly balances do not roll over to subsequent quarters.
2. **Reimbursement Code**: Claims must be submitted via `OrbitPeople` under expense claim code `WELL-300`.
3. **Eligible Expenses**: Eligible expenses include commercial gym memberships, home exercise equipment, ergonomic footwear, meditation subscriptions, and nutritional counseling.

---

## 3. Professional Development and Learning Budget

Every full-time employee is allocated a dedicated professional development budget of $2,500.00 per fiscal year under program code `EDU-BUD-25`. Allowable uses include technical conference registrations, academic course tuition, specialized textbooks, and professional engineering certification exam fees. Core collaborative working hours across all teams are established from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in each employee's local timezone.
