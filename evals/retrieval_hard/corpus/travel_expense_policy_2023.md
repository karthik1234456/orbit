# Corporate Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy (Archived 2023)

> **STATUS: SUPERSEDED / OUTDATED POLICY - FOR HISTORICAL AUDIT ONLY**
> 
> *Notice: This document reflects corporate travel expense guidelines in effect between January 1, 2023 and December 31, 2023 under policy code `EXP-OLD-2023`. For all current travel bookings and expense filings, refer to `travel_expense_policy_2026.md`.*

---

## 1. Scope and Core Guidelines

All full-time and contract personnel traveling on behalf of ORBIT must adhere to standard fiscal limits established by finance. Travel requests must be submitted through legacy booking platform `LegacyTravel` using client account routing code `TRV-901`. Any booking made outside `TRV-901` requires manual routing via finance code `FIN-APP-01`.

Employees should exercise prudent judgment when incurring travel expenses. Business travel must be pre-authorized by an immediate team lead at least 14 calendar days prior to departure.

---

## 2. Daily Per Diem Allowances

Under policy `EXP-OLD-2023`, daily meal per diems are strictly fixed at $45.00 per day for domestic travel. International per diems vary by country tier. Alcohol and tobacco purchases are strictly non-reimbursable.

| Category | Daily Maximum Allowance | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Breakfast | $10.00 | Only if hotel does not provide complimentary breakfast |
| Lunch | $15.00 | Midday meal during business hours |
| Dinner | $20.00 | Evening meal while on travel status |
| **Total Daily Per Diem** | **$45.00** | Maximum combined daily limit |

---

## 3. Lodging and Ground Transportation

Hotel accommodations must be selected from standard tier corporate preferred hotels:

- **Tier 1 Major Metro Lodging Cap**: Maximum allowable rate is $160.00 per night excluding taxes (applies to New York, San Francisco, London, and Tokyo).
- **Standard Region Lodging Cap**: Maximum allowable rate is $110.00 per night excluding taxes.
- **Mileage Reimbursement**: Personal vehicle mileage reimbursement rate is $0.54 per mile.
- **Ground Transit**: Standard taxi fares, airport shuttle buses, and rideshare rides are reimbursable between transit hubs and hotel.

---

## 4. Documentation and Approval Thresholds

Employees must submit expense claims via the legacy Concur portal `CONCUR-2023` within 30 days of trip conclusion:

1. **Receipt Requirement Threshold**: Itemized receipts are required only for individual expenses exceeding $50.00.
2. **Manager Approval Limit**: Department manager approval is required only when total expense report exceeds $1,000.00.
3. **Cash Advances**: Advances are capped at $200.00 cash per trip upon written request to payroll.
