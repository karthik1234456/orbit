# Remote Work, Hybrid Attendance, and Office Environment Standards

> **WORKPLACE POLICY: POL-REM-2026**
> 
> *Issued by: People Operations & Workplace Experience | Scope: All Global Locations*

---

## 1. Hybrid Attendance Schedules and Anchor Days

ORBIT embraces a flexible hybrid operational model designed to balance in-person collaboration with focused remote productivity:

| Work Arrangement Tier | Residency Criteria | Mandatory Office Attendance | Approval Authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier R1: Fully Remote | Residing >30 miles from corporate hub | 0 days per week (optional visits) | Department Vice President |
| Tier R2: Standard Hybrid | Residing ≤30 miles from corporate hub | 2 anchor days per week (Tuesdays & Thursdays) | Direct People Manager |
| Tier R3: On-Site Essential | Lab researchers and datacenter technicians | 4 to 5 days per week on-site | Facilities Operations Director |

Employees assigned to Tier R2 must maintain attendance on mandatory anchor days (every Tuesday and Thursday) at Headquarters Building A (`HQ-BLD-A`).

---

## 2. Home Workstation Setup Stipend

To promote ergonomic health in home working environments, new hires receive a one-time home office setup reimbursement of up to $1,000.00:

1. **Submission Code**: Invoices for ergonomic equipment must be submitted to the finance portal under claim code `REM-SET-50` within the first 60 days of employment.
2. **Covered Hardware**: Eligible purchases include motorized standing desks, ergonomic office chairs, noise-canceling headsets, and anti-fatigue floor mats.
3. **Asset Ownership**: Equipment procured using `REM-SET-50` remains the property of the employee upon departure.

---

## 3. Hot-Desking Reservation and Office Etiquette

Workstation allocation operates on an agile hot-desking model managed through the software reservation system `AgilQuest DeskFlow` at intranet address `orbit.corp/desks`:

- **Reservation Lead Time**: Desks must be reserved at least 12 hours prior to arrival.
- **Daily Quiet Hours**: To facilitate deep engineering focus, silent hours are observed daily from 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM across open-plan floors 2 through 5 in Building A.
- **Visitor Pre-Registration**: External guests and vendors must be registered at least 24 hours in advance through the online portal `visitor-kiosk.orbit.corp`.
