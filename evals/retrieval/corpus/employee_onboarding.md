# New Employee Onboarding and Orientation Handbook

Welcome to the organization! The first week is structured to ensure a smooth transition, seamless systems access, and an introduction to our workplace culture and operating principles.

On day one, arriving employees report to the main lobby at 9:00 AM to meet their dedicated onboarding concierge. Security personnel will issue your permanent photo identification badge and two-factor authentication security keys. Your hiring manager will welcome you, introduce immediate team colleagues, and guide you to your designated workstation where your pre-configured computer hardware will be ready for setup.

During the initial three days, new hires must complete the required administrative checklist in the human resources portal. This includes enrolling in payroll direct deposit, electing group health and dental insurance benefits, designating emergency contact persons, and acknowledging the employee code of conduct and workplace safety standards.

By Thursday of the first week, your mentor will assist you in establishing your software development environment, configuring repository permissions, and obtaining access credentials for our continuous integration systems. All new hires are paired with an experienced peer buddy who provides guidance, answers operational questions, and facilitates introductions across adjacent teams throughout the first ninety days of employment.

Orientation concludes on Friday afternoon with an executive leadership welcome session and a casual team lunch. If you encounter any technical impediments with system permissions or access cards, contact the workplace helpdesk immediately.
