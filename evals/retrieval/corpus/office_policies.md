# Standard Office Attendance and Flexible Work Policy

The standard working day begins between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM, with core collaboration hours established between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM local time. All salaried employees are expected to maintain presence during these core hours either physically on site or via active status on the team messaging platform.

Flexible work arrangements permit eligible team members to work remotely up to two days per week, subject to prior coordination with their designated department manager. Remote days must be scheduled at least one week in advance in the company scheduling calendar. Mondays and Thursdays are designated as preferred anchor days where in-person presence is strongly encouraged for cross-functional alignment meetings.

When visiting or working from an office location other than your primary assigned facility, desks must be reserved through the workplace portal at least twenty-four hours before arrival. Hot-desking stations provide dual monitor displays, universal USB-C docking hubs, and ergonomic task seating. Access badges must be worn and kept clearly visible above the waist at all times within office perimeters.

Visitors and external contractors must register at the reception lobby upon arrival. The visiting sponsor remains responsible for escorting guests throughout their visit and ensuring temporary guest passes are surrendered to building security upon departure. Any temporary badge loss must be reported immediately to workplace operations.
