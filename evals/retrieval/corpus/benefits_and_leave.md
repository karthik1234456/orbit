# Employee Health Benefits, Wellness Programs, and Leave Allocations

Our comprehensive employee benefits package is designed to support the physical health, mental well-being, and work-life balance of every team member and their dependents.

Full-time employees accrue twenty days of paid time off per calendar year, calculated on a pro-rata basis each bi-weekly pay cycle. A maximum of five unused paid time off days may be rolled over into the following calendar year; any additional unused balance is forfeited on December 31. Time off requests exceeding three consecutive business days must be submitted through the human resources portal at least two weeks in advance for manager approval.

In addition to standard paid time off, the company provides twelve paid company holidays and two floating personal days. Parental leave offers sixteen weeks of fully paid leave for primary caregivers and eight weeks for secondary caregivers following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Parental leave benefits may be utilized continuously or taken in multi-week increments during the first twelve months following the qualifying family event.

The corporate wellness stipend provides an annual reimbursement allowance of six hundred dollars for qualified health expenses, including fitness club memberships, home gym equipment, ergonomics consultations, and mindfulness application subscriptions. Reimbursement requests must be accompanied by itemized payment receipts submitted through the benefits reimbursement portal within ninety days of purchase.
