# Corporate Travel and Business Expense Reimbursement Rules

All business-related expenses incurred on behalf of the company must adhere to established fiduciary principles, reasonable financial prudence, and timely documentation submission.

Business travel requiring flights or overnight hotel accommodations must be booked through the corporate travel portal at least fourteen days in advance of departure. Economy class airfare is mandatory for all domestic flights under six hours in duration. Lodging nightly rates are capped at designated regional per diem allowances published annually on the finance department portal.

Meal reimbursements operate under a daily allowance of seventy-five dollars for domestic travel and one hundred dollars for international destinations. Alcohol expenditures are not reimbursable under standard meal allocations. Itemized receipts are required for all individual expenses exceeding twenty-five dollars; credit card summary charge slips without line-item itemization will be rejected during audit.

Expense reports must be submitted through the finance reimbursement software within thirty calendar days of expense incurrence or travel completion. Reimbursement claims between five hundred and two thousand dollars require written signoff from the direct department manager. Claims exceeding two thousand dollars require secondary review and authorization by the finance controller.

Personal charges, vehicle traffic fines, and entertainment streaming subscriptions are non-reimbursable. Approved reimbursements are processed via direct deposit in the bi-weekly payroll cycle following report approval.
