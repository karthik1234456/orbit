# Standard Workstation Hardware and Peripheral Specifications

Every full-time team member receives standard computing hardware configured according to job responsibilities. Software engineers and data scientists are provisioned with enterprise workstation laptops equipped with a 14-core processor, 64 gigabytes of unified memory, and a 1 terabyte solid-state drive. Non-engineering roles are allocated ultralight productivity notebooks featuring an 8-core processor, 32 gigabytes of memory, and 512 gigabytes of storage.

Standard desk packages include dual 27-inch 4K monitors, an adjustable ergonomic monitor mounting arm, a high-definition video conferencing camera with physical privacy shutter, a noise-canceling stereo headset, and a universal Thunderbolt docking station capable of delivering 100 watts of charging power. Wireless mechanical keyboards and precision mice are available from the hardware inventory upon request.

Hardware replacement cycles operate on a thirty-six month schedule for portable computers and a sixty month schedule for external displays. If equipment experiences accidental damage or hardware failure prior to normal lifecycle expiration, team members should submit a support ticket accompanied by managerial endorsement. Damaged peripherals such as charging bricks, adapter dongles, and damaged patch cables may be swapped at any local IT service kiosk without advance appointment.

All corporate computing devices are enrolled in automated management software to enforce storage volume encryption, anti-malware definitions, and operating system security patches. Unauthorized hardware modifications or personal secondary operating system installations are strictly prohibited.
