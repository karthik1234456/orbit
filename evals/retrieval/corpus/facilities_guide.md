# Facilities Operations, Building Amenities, and Parking Guidelines

The corporate campus facilities are engineered to provide a comfortable, sustainable, and productive environment for all staff members, contractors, and authorized visitors.

Main exterior building doors unlock for badge holders Monday through Friday from 6:30 AM until 8:00 PM. Outside these standard hours and throughout weekends, building entry requires badge authentication at designated perimeter security turnstiles. The campus security control center operates continuously twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week.

Employee vehicle parking is permitted in parking structure North and surface lot West. Electric vehicle charging stations are situated on levels one and two of parking structure North; charging sessions are limited to four consecutive hours to allow equitable access for all electric vehicle owners. Secure indoor bicycle cages and locker rooms equipped with private showers are accessible on the basement level of the main building.

Conference rooms range from four-person huddle spaces to the sixty-person auditorium. Rooms must be reserved through the workplace calendar system. If a booked conference room remains unoccupied fifteen minutes after the scheduled reservation start, the booking automatically cancels and the room becomes available for impromptu walk-up use.

The campus cafeteria serves breakfast between 7:30 AM and 9:30 AM, and lunch between 11:30 AM and 2:00 PM. Complimentary coffee, tea, and healthy snacks are stocked daily in kitchen pantries on every floor.
